What do I need to provide? +
Just access to a water spigot and an electrical outlet near where your car is parked. Shade is preferred but we bring a canopy when needed. We supply all equipment, products, and water filtration.
How long does a detail take? +
A Standard Detail runs 2.5–3.5 hours. Premium takes 4–6. Ceramic coating may span two days. We'll give you an accurate estimate when you book based on your vehicle's size and condition.
How long does ceramic coating last? +
Our standard coating lasts 2 years. Premium is 5 years. Both provide UV protection that's critical in Florida — the ceramic layer prevents paint fade and oxidation from the intense sun and salt air.
What products do you use? +
Professional-grade products from Gtechniq, CarPro, Gyeon, Koch Chemie, and Rupes. No off-the-shelf consumer products. Our ceramic coatings are certified professional-only formulations not available to the public.
What's your cancellation policy? +
24-hour notice for cancellations or rescheduling at no charge. Within 24 hours may be subject to a $50 trip fee. We're always flexible with weather-related reschedules — it's Florida, we get it.
